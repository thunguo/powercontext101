## This page will cover

- `PowerContext` Capability: three tools, automatic recall per run, and optional capture / flush
- `PowerContextToolset`: the same three explicit tools without automatic lifecycle behavior
- Per-run Scope callbacks, history replacement, and privacy boundaries
- Automatic fail-open behavior versus explicit-tool `ModelRetry`
- The current lack of Handoff, Review, and direct Experience / Skill operations
